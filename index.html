Who I Am

Bridging science
and compassion

I am a pre-medical student at the University of Puerto Rico, pursuing a degree in Biomedical Sciences with a focus on applying to medical school and becoming a physician. My academic journey is defined by a relentless pursuit of understanding — from molecular biology to the human experience of illness.

Beyond medicine, I explore the intersections of pharmacy, academic research, and the possibility of a PhD. I believe the best doctors are those who never stop asking why.
