Academic Work

Research &
scholarly work

A selection of ongoing and completed research projects spanning molecular biology, clinical sciences, and pharmacology.

01 Parasitology

First Findings of Plasmodium Parasite Presence in Anoles in a Northern Subtropical Moist Forest in Puerto Rico

A field parasitology study documenting the first recorded presence of Plasmodium parasites in anole lizards within a northern subtropical moist forest ecosystem in Puerto Rico, contributing baseline data on host–parasite dynamics in the region. Presented at the SACNAS NDiSTEM Conference (October 2023) with the support of a SACNAS Travel Scholarship.

2023 Completed
02 Physiology

Investigating the Role of Oxidative Stress and Sex Differences in an Ischemia/Reperfusion Model in Mice

An experimental physiology study examining how oxidative stress and biological sex influence cellular damage and recovery in a murine model of ischemia–reperfusion injury.

2024 Completed
03 Pharmacology

Comparison of Cell Death Activation in Advanced Prostate Cancer by Novel ABQ-48 and BQ-108 Compounds

A pharmacology study comparing how the novel compounds ABQ-48 and BQ-108 activate programmed cell death pathways in advanced prostate cancer cells, evaluating their potential as therapeutic candidates.
